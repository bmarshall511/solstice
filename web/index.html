About panel-level datasystem-level

Each of your 30 SunPower panels has its own Enphase IQ7XS microinverter, so there are no strings: every panel tracks its own maximum power point and tops out at 315 W AC. Tesla's meter only sees the array's total, so Solstice compares that total against the sunlight hitting your roof, which is enough to catch dust, shading changes or a failed section of the array. Per-panel readings would need the SunPower monitoring box (PVS) on your wall.
