Mockup y · Where your energy goes

A typical day, split into what Solstice can see.

Solstice measures the AC (2.7 kW while it runs) and the pool. The rest of the house splits two ways: an always-on base from the quietest hours of the night, and big short loads that look like the water heater, the dryer and the oven. A new card in Insights › Home shows the split, today's big loads, and how the always-on base trends. Numbers are illustrative.

New: a card between "Heat & your AC" and "Overnight baseline". It has a total, one stacked bar and five parts, each with kWh a day, a share, and a line on how it's measured:

  • AC: cooling time × 2.7 kW, measured.
  • Pool: measured.
  • Always-on: the quietest stretch of each night × 24 h.
  • Big loads: bursts of 3 kW or more above the base, lasting 15 minutes or more, while the AC and the pool pump are off.
  • Everything else: what's left.

The switch at the top chooses the last 7 days, today, or the last 30 days. Tap a part to open it (frame 2).

Unchanged: every other card in Insights › Home.

New push, in the Unusual usage alert type you already have:

  • When: 3 nights in a row with the always-on base 0.3 kW or more above its 30-day median.
  • How often: once, then not again until the base has come back down.
  • Where it goes: sent at 7 AM, opening this card.

How each part is worked out

  • AC: Nest cooling (or heating) time × the learned draw (2.7 kW, from 320 clean switches). Measured.
  • Pool: the existing pool figure (pump watts by reading, plus UV and extras).
  • Always-on: the lowest 30-minute average of Tesla's home power between 1 and 5 AM, with the AC and the pump off, × 24 h.
  • Big loads: 5-minute buckets at least 3 kW above that base for 15 minutes or more, with the AC and the pool pump off. Only the energy above the base counts. Labelled "looks like" a heater, dryer or oven, never as a certainty.
  • Everything else: home − the four above: lights, the stovetop, TVs, small appliances.
  • Unchanged: every other card. The History "Where every kWh went" card gets the corrected AC figure automatically.
