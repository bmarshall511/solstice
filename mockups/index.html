Solstice: four directions

Each mockup runs on simulated Powerwall and solar data shaped like the Fleet API live_status and calendar_history responses. Open each one full-screen and drag the 3D scenes. You can mix and match: a layout from one and a hero visual from another.

A · Orbit

Your house, live, in 3D

A 3D model of your home with glowing particle streams showing energy moving between solar, Powerwall, home and grid in real time. The sun tracks the time of day, and glass panels sit over the scene.
B · Mission Control

Dense, analytical, desktop-first

A 3D "production landscape" of 30 days × 24 hours, with underperforming days flagged in red. Around it: live KPIs with sparklines, a system-health panel, alerts and a forecast.
C · Aurora

Calm, glanceable, phone-first

A liquid-filled 3D Powerwall orb in front of an aurora sky that changes color with your energy mix. Plain-language insights and a radial history chart, in an installable PWA.
D · Rooftop Twin

Your house, panels and Powerwall

A 3D model of your roof lit by the real sun position and live Open-Meteo weather. Shows shading on each panel and an irradiance heat map. Scrub through today or tomorrow. (Local only, not in the repo.)

Every mockup has an All data tab on the right edge (or press D). It shows every Fleet API field, today's energy flows, Powerwall health, outages, history, rates and your electric bills.

All numbers are simulated. The efficiency alert ("11% below expected") is a built-in example of the detection we'll run on your real data against Open-Meteo irradiance.
