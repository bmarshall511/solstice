New alerts, only while a trip runs:

  • grid down
  • Powerwalls under 30% while the grid is down
  • inside over 88° or under 50°
  • humidity over 65% for 6 h
  • thermostat offline over 1 h
  • the pump not running in a scheduled hour (read-only check), or a pump error
  • 2 kW or more for 15 min that isn't the AC, pool or water heater
  • a change at the thermostat or pool panel
  • a storm raise

Each goes through the existing alert settings and limits. The grid-down text here replaces the usual "turn the AC off" advice, since nobody is there.

"Someone's allowed" keeps the trip going and doesn't ask again that day.

Held until you're back, then sent as one summary: the water-test reminder, dusty panels, and "relay gone quiet". There's no daily "all fine" message.

New: on the arrival day the banner changes to the welcome-home plan.

The setpoint steps: 2° each, one every 30 min (the existing guard). They aim at your day target, or your night target if you arrive after 10 PM.

Start time: when to start comes from how fast this house cools at the forecast temperature. On a 95° day that's about 5 h ahead. Each trip measures it again. Spare solar is used first; otherwise it starts on time anyway, so you walk in within 1° of the target.

Pool: its normal plan was written Saturday 8:15 PM.

Early: "I'm home", Nest going Home, or a change at the thermostat or pool panel ends the trip at once. The AC plan resumes and the pool goes back to normal at the next 8:15 PM run.

Late: "Running late" takes a new time. With no sign of you 2 h after the arrival time, it goes back to 85° and asks whether to extend.

Measured: 1.6–2° an hour on a September evening, and about 1.3° an hour rise with the AC idle on a 90° afternoon.

The rules

  • Only your tap starts a trip. Detection asks; it never starts one.
  • Inside a trip each system follows its Autopilot, which is Auto for both today.
  • The storm rule acts without a tap only during a trip. It only ever raises the reserve (to at least 20%, one change an hour) and puts it back by itself afterwards.
  • Limits. They live in guards.ts, as now:
    • AC cooling: 80–85° while away, 2° a step, one write per 30 min.
    • Heat setback: 50–60° (new). The trip uses 55°.
    • Pool: Pool and High Speed programs only, with a goal of 1–1.5 turnovers. Never the spa, lights, heater or freeze protection; the controller's own freeze protection keeps running the pump in a freeze.
    • Departure check: it can turn things off, and only when you tap.
  • Everything reverts when the trip ends, whether at the date, early or late, or by "I'm home":
    • the AC plan comes back and the pool returns to its normal goal
    • the storm rule goes back to Suggest
    • held pushes arrive as one summary
    • guests come back after 24 h
    • learning resumes
    Each step goes in the AC and pool logs.
  • Fixed first (found in the audit):
    • guest reads on an Away day no longer claim AC control days
    • Autopilot no longer sends a setpoint while Nest is in Eco
    • freeze-protection runs no longer count as your pump runs
  • Unchanged: comfort targets, holds, the planner's normal goal, Clear-up, the Powerwall export rule and reserve floor, and every card not listed in frame 7.
