The rules

  • Clear day: under 15% midday cloud and at least 4 kWh/m² of sun on the panel plane; output is divided by that sunlight and adjusted to 25 °C panels (−0.35% per °C).
  • Clean level: the median of the first 3 clear days after a rain of 5 mm or more (or after you log a cleaning). Now: the median of the latest 3 clear days, from the 6th clear day on.
  • Dusty at 5% or more below clean (replayed: twice in 13 months, both real; 7% never fired). The push also needs no 5 mm rain in the 5-day forecast.
  • Read only: Tesla history and Open-Meteo (the archive for past days, the forecast for rain). No new device access.
  • Unchanged: the card's layout, the cleaning log and Undo, the panel grid, the per-panel "panel low" check, and every other card.
