The rules

  • Always-on: identical to "Where your energy goes" (quietest tenth of 1–5 AM with the AC off, less the pump), so the two cards always agree.
  • Other / not split: what's left of the night's average after the three parts, never below zero. Nights without Nest data are always-on plus "not split".
  • Unchanged: the card's place on the page, its title, the 60-night range, and every other card. Guests see the same split with no extra detail.
