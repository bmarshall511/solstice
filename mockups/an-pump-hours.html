A pump-hours goal

The pump reports no flow, so every turnover figure is modelled. Pump hours are measured: the controller says when the pump runs. This adds a second way to set the pool goal, in hours a day, next to turnovers.

Only the Pool Autopilot card and the Pool tiles change. Turnovers stay the default until you switch.

Choices I made (say if you'd like them different)

  • Speed in hours mode is your saved filter speed (Pool sheet presets), not one the planner picks, so the goal means exactly "this many hours at this speed".
  • Vacation trips keep their own turnover goal (1 or 1.5 a day) whichever goal you set at home.
  • Hours mode is off until you switch it; switching back to Turnovers restores your turnover goal unchanged.
