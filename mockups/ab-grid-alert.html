Unchanged: these two switches are already there. They now control the three pushes: the first covers grid down and grid back, the second the 30% warning.

The rules

  • Source: the 5-minute check that already reads Tesla's live status for the storm watch. No extra Tesla calls.
  • Fresh readings only: a reading older than 10 minutes never starts or ends an outage. If Tesla stops answering, nothing is sent (the Now screen's amber "Last reading … ago" chip from mockup x covers that).
  • One push each: one "down" and one "back" per outage, one 30% warning per outage; at most 6 grid pushes a day. A blip shorter than 5 minutes may be missed between checks; Tesla's own outage history still records it.
  • Read-only: nothing is written to the Powerwalls, the pool or the AC.
  • Unchanged: the Now screen's outage mode and its toast, the outage view, and every other alert.
