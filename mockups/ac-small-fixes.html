New: the muted grey used for small labels and footnotes goes from 50% to 60% white. Today it reads at 4.9:1 on the darkest cards but drops to 3.7:1 over the lighter aurora, under the 4.5:1 small text needs; at 60% that becomes 4.6:1 (6.6:1 on the darkest). It is one colour token, so every muted label in the app gets a touch brighter; nothing else changes.
