An expand button on the Next 48 hours road (Now) and the Production landscape (History) opens that scene in a full-screen sheet. In the sheet you pinch to zoom, pan with two fingers, drive or rotate with one finger, and tap for the readout. The close button takes you back to the page. The cards keep their current design.

Interaction rules

  • Zoom limits. Road: 1× (the opening view) to 4×. Landscape: 1× (all 30 days fit) to 6×. Pinch, the mouse wheel and +/− all zoom toward the point under the fingers or cursor. Panning is clamped so the road or grid can never leave the screen. Road: from 3 h before now to the last hour, and from the floor to the top of the battery wall. Landscape: the target stays over the grid.
  • One finger vs two in the sheet. The sheet has no page behind it to scroll, so its scene uses touch-action:none. One finger drives the road sideways through time, or rotates the landscape. When a second finger lands, the one-finger drive or rotate stops at once and that touch becomes a pinch plus pan around the midpoint of the two fingers. Lifting one finger does not restart the drive. A new drive needs all fingers up first, so the view never jumps. A tap counts only if the finger moved less than 6 px and no second finger touched down.
  • Cards stay as they are. On the cards, one finger still drives the road or orbits the landscape sideways, and vertical drags still scroll the page. Pinch still belongs to the browser there. The page's touch helper (lib/touchorbit.js: touchIntent, touchOrbit) is not changed. The road sheet uses its own pointer handler. The landscape sheet uses OrbitControls' own touch mapping (one finger rotate, two fingers dolly plus pan) and never calls touchOrbit.
  • Open and close. Close with the × button, Escape, or the browser/OS back gesture (the sheet adds one history entry). The sheet opens at the card's position and tapped hour or bar. On close it hands back the drive position and the tapped item. Zoom and pan are not handed back, because the card never zooms. While the sheet is open, the card stops rendering and the page stops scrolling. On close, the sheet's WebGL context is disposed, so only one scene renders at a time.
  • Reduced motion and Calm. With Calm on, or the system's reduce-motion setting, the sheet appears and disappears with no slide, the view jumps with no easing (zoom, pan, drive and reset), the hint disappears after 3 s with no fade, and the landscape never auto-rotates. Otherwise the sheet slides up in 240 ms, and drive, zoom and reset ease with the card's existing damping.
  • Hint. It shows every time the sheet opens and goes away after 3 s or at the first touch, whichever comes first. It is never a blocking overlay; it has pointer-events:none.
  • Keyboard. Arrows drive or rotate, +/− zoom, 0 resets the view, Escape closes, and focus returns to the expand button.

Illustrative data only (a Tuesday at 10 AM with the Powerwalls at 57%, and a synthetic 30-day landscape). No prices, bills or location. Every frame is a live three.js render you can also try with a mouse: drag to drive or rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
