The rules

  • Measured nightly from evening discharges: 2 h or more of steady discharge that lowers the charge by 25% or more; energy out ÷ charge used.
  • The figure used is the median of the last 90 days with at least 5 measurements; until then, nameplate × 95% as today.
  • A fade: if a month's median falls 5% below the year's, Insights says so on this card (no push).
  • Unchanged: the reserve, Storm Watch, the Powerwall rules, and every other card.
