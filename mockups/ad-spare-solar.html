Use solar only when it really is spare: Powerwalls full and power going out to PEC.

Your last 12 months: spare solar on 2–12 days a month in spring, fall and winter, none June to September, and 295 kWh sent to PEC in the whole year. So this is small, honest housekeeping, not a money-maker: it stops the AC "pre-cooling on solar" with energy the Powerwalls were saving for the evening, and puts the little real surplus into the pool. The rules

  • Spare = Powerwalls at 95% or more and exporting at least 1 kW, averaged over 15 minutes, from the live readings the 5-minute check already takes. No extra Tesla calls.
  • Pool speed-up only in Auto, only while the Pool program is running, never during a Clear-up or a boost you started, at most once an hour, through the same safety guard as every pool write. It ends when the export drops under 0.3 kW for 15 minutes or the program ends.
  • AC pre-cool uses the same "spare" test; its depth, timing and limits stay as they are.
  • Unchanged: the turnover planner and its daytime window, the AC comfort band, the Powerwall rules, and every other card.
