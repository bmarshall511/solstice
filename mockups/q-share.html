Mockup · share view

Live energy for guests. Money stays with the owner.

What someone sees when the owner sends them a link. It is the same app with the same tabs and card heights. Every dollar amount becomes a frosted decoy, the three money cards on History are locked, and controls that write to the pool or the thermostat are hidden. Below that: the owner's Sharing group and sheets, the three sign-in cards, and the Frost wipe the owner sees when he previews the guest view.

What's real vs mocked

Layout, classes and copy come from the app and the share-view design (§1.4, §4, §5, §7). All numbers are synthetic but shaped like this home: about 76 kWh a day, 49% from solar, 2 × Powerwall 2, pool pump 153 W at 1,500 RPM. The veils are decoys, so no dollar value exists on this page. The owner's own figures in the Frost demo are shown as placeholders. The orb, the energy-flow house and the pool twin are flat stand-ins here, because the share view does not change those 3D scenes. The QR pattern is illustrative.

Tap any veil or locked card to see the privacy toast.

7Owner preview · the Frost wipe

The owner flips "Preview as a guest". Frost grows from the switch over 450 ms. At 300 ms, under the frost, the app refetches as a guest. From 750 to 1350 ms the frost clears from the top down, and each veil fades in as it is uncovered. Owner-only rows disappear, "Shared with you" appears and the preview pill drops in. Exit runs it backwards: frost rises from the bottom, the owner data reloads, then the frost clears from the top. With Calm mode on (try the switch in the demo) or reduced motion, a 150 ms crossfade replaces the sweep.
