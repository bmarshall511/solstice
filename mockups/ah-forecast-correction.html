Mockup ah · Forecast correction

Three fixes the data asked for, and one it said to leave alone.

I backtested the forecasts on 197 days, using Open-Meteo's archived day-ahead weather, so each day only uses what was known the evening before. Feeding the error back in ("bias correction") made solar worse (±5.6 → ±6.1 kWh a day). What helps is fixing the specific causes below. The figures are your real ones.

Finding · Solar stays as is

No change: over 197 days the solar forecast is off by ±5.6 kWh a day (19%) with almost no bias (−0.7). The misses on 10/1–10/4 were the weather forecast: the day before, Open-Meteo forecast 2.7, 2.5, 2.1 and 1.8 kWh/m² of sun; 1.8, 1.2, 0.9 and 1.6 arrived. Four corrections I tried (bias feedback, yield by sky type, a curve for dark days, a 14-day multiplier) were all worse on those days and no better overall.

The row's ±58% is per hour (dawn and dusk hours make it large); per day it's ±19%. That label isn't changed here.

The rules

  • Home use: the day's total = a line fitted to the last 14 days' use against each day's high (needs 10 days and a spread of at least 6°; otherwise the 14-day average, as now); the hours keep the 14-day shape. The Now tab's Next 48 hours, the outage sim and the reserve rule all use it.
  • Always-on: the median of the last 7 nights with thermostat readings (needs 5).
  • Pool scoring: days with a Clear-up, your runs or outside runs are skipped and shown dashed.
  • Unchanged: solar, the battery % model, every other model, the report's layout and the confidence rules.
