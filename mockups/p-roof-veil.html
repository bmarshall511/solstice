Dust you can see, hours you can check.

The Live roof card stays as it is: house, panels and Powerwalls, the sun-view camera and the dashed sun path. Three things are added. A faint dust veil lies over every panel, and its strength follows the Cleaning check's dust score (55 here). An "Hour bars" chip, off by default, shows 15 hours (6 AM to 8 PM) as thin bar pairs standing in a row on the lawn below the panels, morning on the left: white is what the sunlight could have made, gold is what the panels made. The HUD gets one more line comparing the sun's offer with the panels' output. On a clear, cool day, when an hourly mean reaches 9.2 kW, that line switches to the flat-topping note, and a faint line over the bars marks the 9.45 kW ceiling.

What's real vs mocked

All numbers are synthetic but shaped like the home: 9.6 kW DC, 30 microinverters topping out at 9.45 kW AC, 94–98°F September highs. In the app the veil's alpha comes from the Cleaning check score, "sun says" is Open-Meteo tilted irradiance × the learned yield, and "panels" is the hourly mean of Tesla's 5-minute readings. Flat-topping is judged on hourly means only, because single 5-minute readings run high.

The slider only drives this mockup (it redraws the panel texture on both frames). In the app the texture is redrawn only when the Performance card refreshes.
