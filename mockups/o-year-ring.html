A whole year on one ring.

A new History card after the production landscape. Every day of the last 365 is one bar on three rings: solar on the outside, home use in the middle, energy bought from PEC on the inside. Green ticks on the floor mark days the Powerwalls reached 100%. Red beads mark outages, sized by how long they lasted. A faint gold line outside the solar ring traces last year's solar, only for the days where last year's data exists. The white hand is today. Tap any day to read it, then open it in the Day view.

What's real vs mocked

All 437 days here are synthetic, shaped like the home: about 76 kWh a day, solar covering about 49%, a summer AC hump with 94–98°F highs, a winter dip, 2 × Powerwall 2 with a 20% reserve and the pool pump at 153 W from spring to fall. In the app the rings read the daily history, outages and highs already loaded on History. The ghost fetches 800 days once when the card first builds, and it is not part of the 5-minute refresh.

"Open this day →" would switch History to Day for that date. Here it only shows a note.
