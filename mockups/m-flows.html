Every kWh, from where it came to where it went.

A new History card placed directly after the totals. It follows the Day / Week / Month / Year selector. Sources are on the left: solar, PEC and the Powerwalls discharging. Sinks are on the right: the home, the Powerwalls charging and export to PEC. Ribbon width is kWh. Colour runs from the source to the sink. The Home slab is split into pool, AC and everything else, and each part says where its number comes from. Tap a ribbon or a slab to highlight it. Tap Home again to step through AC, pool and the rest. Move the pointer, or drag sideways, to tilt the view up to 12°.

What's real vs mocked

Real in Phase 2: one read-only /api/flows query that sums Tesla's stored 5-minute rows. Pool kWh comes from ScreenLogic readings, or from the schedule when readings are sparse. AC kWh comes from Nest cooling minutes × the learned kW, or from the heat model. Mocked here: every number, shaped like a hot September day and a 30-day month for this home. The $ uses a sample rate.
