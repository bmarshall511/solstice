How well Solstice knows your home.

Every model Solstice runs — solar yield, the AC's kW, the pump curve, the 48-hour forecast, battery capacity — now carries a badge for how much to trust it: measured, learned ±X%, estimated ±X%, learning · n of N, or unscored. This mockup stacks the new "How well Solstice knows your home" report card, its tap-through raw-data sheet, the confidence badges dropped onto five existing cards, two persisted anomaly cards, the Data health row and the AC Autopilot trim suggestion — all as they'd appear in Insights → Home and nearby cards. Nothing here writes to ScreenLogic or Nest; the action buttons are inert in this mockup.

What's real vs mocked

All numbers are illustrative and shaped like the design doc's example (76 kWh/day home, 2 Powerwalls, 2.0 kW learned AC, 153 W pool pump at 1,500 RPM). Tapping a Model Report row opens a real interactive sheet with a synthetic 30-point scatter. No price, loan, bill or location figures appear anywhere. The badge vocabulary, row layout, sparkline shape and anomaly copy follow the approved design doc §2, §6, §11 and §12.
