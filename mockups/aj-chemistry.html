The rules

  • Ranges shown: free chlorine 1–4 ppm, pH 7.2–7.8, alkalinity 80–120 ppm, CYA 30–50 ppm (common residential guidance). They only color the values; you can change them.
  • Pool only to start; the spa can get its own log later if you test it separately.
  • Nothing is written to the controller. A Clear-up starts only when you tap it.
  • Unchanged: the planner, Autopilot, the Clear-up and every other card.
