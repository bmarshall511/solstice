Mockup u · Panels tab

Every panel, by name and place.

The PVS relay now sends each of the 30 microinverters' readings every 5 minutes. This mockup shows how the Panels tab could use them. The Live roof gets a Per panel chip that tints each panel against the array's median, and tapping a panel pins its readout to the roof. A new Panel health card goes under Performance vs sunlight. Panels are always named by where they sit: Row 1 is the top row at the ridge, Row 3 the eave row, and panels count 1 to 10 from the north end (left in the default roof view). Serial numbers are never shown. All numbers are illustrative.

New: a Per panel chip beside Hour bars (tap to toggle). When it is on, each panel gets a thin tint over its usual glow: blue when it is making less than the array's median panel right now, gold when it is making more, and no tint within ±5%. While it is on, the shared output glow drops to 40% so the tints read against it. A legend line under the chips gives the scale. The HUD gains one line: how many of the 30 microinverters reported in the latest poll, and the weakest panel's share of the median.

Unchanged: the house, panels and Powerwalls, the dust veil, the sun path, the output glow whenever Per panel is off, drag to rotate, the Hour bars chip and its legend, and the existing HUD lines. Nothing new stands on the roof: the tint is a second layer on the same 30 panels.

Rules: each panel's ratio is its latest AC kW divided by the median of the panels that reported in the same poll. The tint deepens from ±5% to full colour at −15% (the anomaly threshold) or +10% (a panel rarely beats the median by more). While the array median is under 20 W the chip is greyed out and reads "sun down". A tap (under 6 px of movement) selects a panel, but only while Per panel is on; a drag still rotates.

New: tapping a panel outlines it in white, pauses the slow auto-rotate and pins a readout to it. At 375 px there is no room beside the panel, so the readout is a strip along the bottom of the scene (or just under the HUD when the panel sits low), tied to the panel by a leader line that follows it as you drag. It shows the panel's name, AC kW out and DC kW in now, heat-sink °C, kWh so far today, and that panel's share of the array's kWh today (an even share would be 3.3%). Under those is a sparkline of today's 5-minute AC output (gold) against the median panel (dashed). ✕ or a tap on empty sky closes it. Tap any panel in frame 1 or 2 to try it.

Unchanged: everything else on the card. The HUD keeps its place top left, and the readout stays clear of it.

Rules: the "now" values come from /api/pvs/latest (kw, kwDc, tempC). The kWh comes from /api/pvs/day, where it is the lifetime-counter difference, and the sparkline uses that route's aligned 5-minute series. A reading older than 15 minutes shows its age ("0.21 kW · 40 min ago") in place of "now".

New: a Panel health card between Performance vs sunlight and Cleaning check. Its 3 × 10 grid of tiles is laid out like the roof (ridge row on top, north end on the left). Each tile shows that panel's kWh today and is tinted by it against the median panel, on the same scale as the roof. Below the grid: the three lowest panels with their deficit, any panel that has stopped reporting, and the date the per-panel data starts. Tapping a tile scrolls up to the Live roof with that panel selected (frame 2).

Unchanged: Performance vs sunlight, Cleaning check and everything else on the tab.

Rules: today's kWh is the latest lifetime counter minus today's first reading, so gaps in the relay lose no energy. The median covers the panels that reported in the last 15 minutes. A panel is not reporting when its newest reading is more than 15 minutes (3 polls) older than the relay's newest poll. It gets a hatched tile and is left out of the median and the lowest-three list. If the whole relay goes silent, the card says "Relay last heard 4:40 PM" and no single panel is blamed.

New: a flagged panel's tile turns red, the card header gets a "1 to check" badge, and a red box names the panel with today's share, its last 7 days and one line of diagnosis from DC in vs AC out. The flag opens a learning-layer anomaly of kind panel.low@r2c7 (severity warn, action open_panels). That puts it on the existing anomaly line in the Learning card and in the weekly digest's "Worth a look", and sends one push of the existing alert kind panel, already capped at 2 in 24 hours.

Unchanged: the Learning card, the digest and Settings › Alerts. The Panel fault switch already exists; this is what finally feeds it.

Rules: the anomaly fires when a panel's kWh is below 85% of the median panel on 5 of the last 7 days on which the array was producing. A producing day is one where the array made at least 15 kWh and the panel reported for at least 90% of daylight polls; other days are skipped, not counted as good. It clears after 3 producing days in a row at 90% or more. Diagnosis: if the panel's DC in is low too, less light is reaching it (shade or soiling). If DC is normal but AC out is under 90% of DC, the microinverter is losing it.

Rules in one place

  • Names: "Row r · c". Row 1 is the ridge row and Row 3 the eave row; c counts 1–10 from the north end. The serial-to-position map lives in the database only (kv pvs:layout), never on screen or in git.
  • Tint: the ratio to the median panel. Within ±5%, no tint. Below that, blue that reaches full strength at −15%. Above it, gold that reaches full strength at +10%. The roof uses kW now and the grid uses kWh today, on the same scale. Red appears only for an open panel.low anomaly, never for a single low reading.
  • Not reporting: the panel's newest reading is more than 15 minutes (3 polls) behind the relay's newest poll, in daylight. The card line appears at 15 minutes, and a panel push follows after 60 minutes of daylight silence. A silent relay is reported as the relay, not as 30 dead panels.
  • Anomaly: below 85% of the median panel on 5 of the last 7 producing days (array ≥ 15 kWh, panel reported ≥ 90% of daylight polls). It clears after 3 producing days in a row at ≥ 90%, and feeds the learning layer's anomaly line, the digest and the existing panel alert.
  • Read only: nothing here writes to the PVS, Tesla, the pool or the thermostat.

Illustrative data. No serial numbers, price, loan, bill or location figures are shown.
