New: a "Powerwall rules" card on Insights › Home, placed directly below Outage readiness. A row of chips at the top shows the current Tesla settings. Then come three rules. Each has the same Off / Suggest / Auto control the pool Autopilot uses, its current suggestion and the reason for it, Apply and a skip button while in Suggest, the last thing that happened, and its limits as chips. A shared Powerwall log ends the card. When the energy_cmds scope is missing, the blue note becomes the amber one with a single Re-connect Tesla link, the Apply buttons are greyed out, and each suggestion shows its Tesla-app steps instead. Tap the chips above to see that state, and a rule in Auto ("Will set 15% at 9 PM. Undo").

Apply asks first ("Set the backup reserve to 15% until 7 AM?"), as the AC plan's Apply does. Switching a rule to Auto also asks first, with that rule's limits in the dialog. Every rule starts in Suggest.

Unchanged: Outage readiness, Heat & your AC, Overnight baseline, the model report, Data health, and the Home tab itself. Settings › Your system › Backup reserve still shows the current value.

Writes: Suggest never writes. Only your Apply tap writes, and at most one tap every 5 minutes is honoured. Auto writes only inside the clamps: reserve 10–100%, one change per hour, never below 20% before a storm, and the export rule only between battery_ok and pv_only. The operating mode and grid charging are never changed. Every write is logged, and the value it replaced is kept for the automatic restore.

Deviation: the brief said "between Outage readiness and the model report". Two cards (Heat & your AC, Overnight baseline) already sit between those two, so the card goes straight below Outage readiness. Say if you want it lower, just above the model report.

The rule for every write

  • Suggest never writes. Each Powerwall rule starts in Suggest. In Suggest, a suggestion, a push or a card never changes a device; only your Apply tap does, after a confirm dialog. Pool and AC Autopilot stay in Auto, as you set them on Sep 25; this mockup does not change them.
  • Auto writes only inside the clamps. Powerwall reserve 10–100%, at most one automatic change per hour, never below 20% while a storm Watch or Warning is ahead. Export rule only between battery_ok and pv_only, never never. Operating mode and grid charging are never changed automatically.
  • Storm timing. Reserve raises happen before the window and on solar. They never happen inside an ERCOT conservation window unless EEA ≥ 2. The previous value is restored 2 h after the later of the all-clear and a stable grid.
  • Presence never writes a device by itself. "Away until…" and Nest Home/Away Assist change Solstice's own settings. The Nest away setpoint follows only through an approved AC plan or AC Autopilot in Auto, inside 65–85°, at most 2° per step, at most once per 30 minutes. ScreenLogic is never touched by presence.
  • Every write is logged with the old value, the new value and who made it (you or Auto). The Auto push tells you how to undo it.
