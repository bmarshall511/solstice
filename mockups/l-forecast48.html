Mockup · Next 48 hours as a road

Drive into tomorrow.

The Now tab's "Next 48 hours" card keeps its title, legend and sentence. Only the chart changes: the SVG becomes a 260 px road. Solar is the near lane, the home's load is the middle lane, and the Powerwalls' charge is the wall at the back, with the 20% reserve dashed across it. The floor darkens at night and under cloud. The strip at the front shows when the pool runs and when the AC pre-cools or coasts. Today's applied windows are solid and tomorrow's planned ones are outlined. Drag sideways to drive into tomorrow, drag up or down to scroll, and tap any hour to see its numbers.

What's real vs mocked

Mocked: every number (a Tuesday at 10 AM, Powerwalls at 57%, a pop-up storm on Wednesday evening). In the app, the lanes come from the same 48-hour forecast the SVG draws today (your usage profile plus Open-Meteo). The reserve comes from Tesla, and cloud and rain from Open-Meteo. The pool windows come from ScreenLogic's applied schedule and Autopilot's plan for tomorrow. The AC windows come from Solstice's plan; tomorrow's hours need a small new field on the AC week. The markers and the sentence use the same values, so they always match.
