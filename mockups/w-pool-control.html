The rules

  • Owner only. Guests see the states, never the switches, sheets or buttons.
  • Every tap is a real write to the controller, confirmed by reading it back. "Sending…" until then; on failure the tile goes back and says why. Taps are debounced.
  • Manual changes stand. A circuit you turn on runs for its timer; Autopilot never turns it off early. Its water counts toward the day's goal.
  • Autopilot's limits stay: it plans only Pool and High Speed, never the spa, heat, lights, Jets, Blower or Waterfall. Your own taps have the controller's limits (450–3,450 RPM, spa 80–104°).
  • Freeze protection is never switched, in either mode.
  • Unchanged: the pool twin, the stats under it, the Autopilot card, the season chart and every other screen.
