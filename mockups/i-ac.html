☀Pre-cool on sunshine

Noon to 5 PM the panels make more than the house uses. Cooling to 72° then stores comfort in the walls and furniture on free power.

▮Coast on the Powerwalls

6 to 9 PM the setpoint drifts to 78°. The house takes about 3 hours to climb 4°, so the AC barely runs when the batteries are carrying the home.

°Every degree is 4%

Your learned 2.5 kWh per degree means each degree of setpoint is worth about 1.5 kWh on a 98° day.

⏱Start early on hot days

On 100°+ days cooling starts at 11 AM so the system never falls behind. Catching up at 6 PM costs more than staying ahead.

⚡Outage and grid mode

Storm Watch, an outage or an ERCOT conservation call: pre-cool, then hold 78° to stretch backup hours.
