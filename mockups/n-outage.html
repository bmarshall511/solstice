What's real vs mocked

Real: the house, panels, Powerwalls and wiring are copied from the app's home scene, the simulation uses the Next 48 hours model's battery step (reserve 0, no grid), and the outage strip is the History card's drawing. Mocked: every number (74% charge, the hourly load profile, tomorrow's 41 kWh, the outage dates), and where the neighbours and the pole sit.
