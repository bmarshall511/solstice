Every system page, one skeleton:

1 · Live · a system card (accent bar, mode, live value, 24-h plan strip) over the scene, then metric tiles
2 · Controls · the equipment panel or thermostat dial; the same component is the sheet from the Now hub
3 · Autopilot · one disclosure card: title · mode · key figure, a summary line; opened: mode segment, goal, rows Schedule · Why · Rules · Log
4 · Analysis · charts with direct labels, disclosure cards

Frames are 393 px wide with 16 px gutters; the dashed amber line on frames 1 and 17 is the bottom of an 852 px screen. The tab bar floats and sticks to the bottom of your window while a frame scrolls past. Caption colours: vs v1 · real · illustrative · motion.

Frame 0 · Component library

vs v1: new. v1 reused the app's existing classes; v2 defines 13 components (prefix c-) in the page's al-components style block, and every later frame is built only from them.

Motion: segments slide their pill with a spring (0.42 s); disclosure chevrons rotate and the body springs open; dial handles snap to the preset ticks with a light haptic; skeletons shimmer, and all of it stops under reduced motion.

Illustrative: the Off-state AC row, the heat-advisory and outage pills, the toggles that are on (Waterfall 1 h left, Pool Light 3 h 10 m) and the spa's 100° (the app's default when the controller reports none).
