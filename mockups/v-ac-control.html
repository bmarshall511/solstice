Mockup v · AC controls and manual hold

Set the thermostat from Solstice, and Autopilot respects what you set.

These frames are the AC half of the decisions from Oct 4. A new Thermostat card has the setpoint, mode, Eco and fan timer. When you change the temperature, here or on the wall, a hold banner says what you set and when Autopilot takes over again. The comfort band becomes editable. Every number is illustrative. Buttons are inert except −/+, the mode switch and the state chips, which show each state.

New: a Thermostat card at the top of the AC panel, above the thermal twin.

−/+ moves the setpoint 1° at a time and sends it 1.5 s after your last tap, so five quick taps are one Nest command. While it is sending, the line under the number reads "Sending 78°…", then "Set · Nest confirmed".

Mode switches between Cool, Heat, Heat · Cool and Off. Changing the mode asks you to confirm.

Eco is a toggle. Fan opens the timer sheet (frame 6).

The green line says what Autopilot is doing right now.

Unchanged: the thermal twin card, the plan card, the Autopilot card and the Home/Away control. They move down by one card and are otherwise identical.

You set it here at 3:52 PM. Autopilot skips its steps until 9:00 PM, the plan's next change ("evening, comfort band"), then goes back to the plan.

New: any setpoint or mode you choose, here or on the wall, starts a hold. It lasts until the plan's next step, but never less than 2 h and never more than 8 h (your choice, Q2).

The banner shows what is held, why and until when, with a thin progress bar.

  • Resume now ends the hold. Autopilot then applies the step due now, still inside its own limits.
  • Hold until morning extends the hold to the plan's 7 AM step.

Writes: your tap sends exactly the value you chose, inside 65–85° cool and 55–80° heat. It isn't limited to 2° per step or one write per 30 minutes, and it doesn't use up Autopilot's 30-minute slot.

How it knows: Solstice keeps the value it last sent. A reading that differs from it, with no Solstice write in between, is a change by someone at home. That works now that Nest's own schedule is off (Q1).

For now it can take up to 5 minutes in the daytime and 15 at night to notice; Google's instant change events come in a later batch (Q3).

A mode change holds the same way. Off or Heat pauses AC Autopilot until the mode is back to Cool (frame 4).

Also fixed: the three bugs that made Solstice undo your changes:

  • a step the thermostat already matched was never marked done
  • the night step was re-applied after midnight
  • the plan was recalculated every 5 minutes; it's now frozen once the day starts

Unchanged: the Autopilot card's layout, mode switch, signals, week chart and log. Hold entries in the log get a lilac dot.

Autopilot only plans cooling. It starts again when the thermostat is back in Cool. Your heat setpoint is yours alone.

Heat shows one setpoint (55–80°). Heat · Cool shows both, as "68 – 76", and −/+ moves the one you last tapped; Nest keeps them at least 3° apart.

Off greys the number out and hides −/+.

Eco shows Nest's eco temperatures. It is the same signal Home/Away Assist uses, so Eco also reads as Away for presence.

In all four states AC Autopilot is paused, and the grey banner says so.

4 of the last 7 nights, each held through the night. Make 74° your night setpoint? Autopilot would plan 74° from 10 PM, and you wouldn't need to change it.

New: when the same kind of change happens on 4 of 7 days, Solstice suggests changing the band. Same kind means a similar time (±1 h), the same direction, and within 1° of each other. Nothing changes unless you tap.

"Not now" hides that suggestion for 14 days.

The rules behind these frames

  • Your taps are yours. Manual setpoints are checked only against 65–85° cool and 55–80° heat. They aren't limited by Autopilot's 2° step or 30-minute rule, and they don't use up Autopilot's 30-minute slot. Taps wait 1.5 s and then send once. That keeps you well inside Google's limit of 5 commands a minute per thermostat.
  • Autopilot's limits don't change. When it writes (and it never writes during a hold), it stays inside 65–85°, moves at most 2° per step and writes at most once per 30 minutes. Off still means no Nest writes at all.
  • A hold lasts until the next plan step, at least 2 h and at most 8 h. Resume now and Hold until morning change that.
  • What ends a hold early: going Away (Nest Eco or Away until…). Marking Home doesn't end one.
  • When the hold ends, Autopilot applies the step due at that time.
  • Every change is logged: who made it (you here, the wall, or Autopilot), the old and new value, and the hold window.
  • Unchanged: the thermal twin, plan chart, savings row, reasons, Autopilot card layout, Home/Away and the Away until… sheet. No pool writes. Pool controls are mockup w.
