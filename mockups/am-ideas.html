New card between the day's tiles and the flow summary. A waterfall splits the change against a typical same weekday into weather, AC beyond the weather, pool, always-on and unexplained. Bars grow right for more and left for less, and the parts add up to the total.

Used · Bought: the segment switches to the change in kWh bought, which is "used more" minus "solar and Powerwalls covered more". That is an exact identity. On a trip day a Trip row appears (owner only). Numbers are illustrative.

Frame 3 · Now: the morning line

From 6 AM to 11 AM the banner slot (lowest priority, below outage, vacation, holds, pool, Powerwall and bill) carries yesterday's change in one line. Why opens frame 1 for yesterday, and Dismiss hides it until tomorrow.

It doesn't show when the change is small (under 2 kWh either way). There's no push, only the banner (recommended).

Frame 4 · Systems › Home: named loads in Where your energy goes (I-22)

Named clusters get their own part and colour, so "Everything else" shrinks. Big loads opens to one row per cluster: its signature (kW · minutes · how often), kWh a day, a 24-hour strip of when it runs, and a suggestion when the shape fits a common appliance.

Badges: never "measured", because this is whole-house inference. "Learned" means named, 20+ runs and steady for 14 days; "estimated" for other named loads; "learning" below 8 runs on 4 days. The parts still add up to the home total. Detection runs on 5-minute data with the AC, pump and always-on taken out, from Sep 25 on (when Nest data starts). Illustrative numbers.

Frame 6 · Systems › AC: the Strip heat card (I-15)

Shown Nov–Mar, or whenever heating was seen in the last 7 days, under the thermostat. Bars are 15-minute heating draw: amber = strips, blue = compressor. It gives the cause (setback recovery or genuine cold) and one tip. There's no button and it writes nothing to the thermostat.

Your heating is detected from the learned heating kW (your choice): under 5 kW reads as a heat pump with strips, otherwise a straight AC heating on strips. Until there are 5 heating runs it says "learning". Last winter's energy-only days are back-tested to set the thresholds before the first cold front (shown "estimated"). Illustrative numbers.

Frame 7 · The push, the alert switch and the digest line

One push at most a day, evaluated at 10 AM after the morning window, so nothing buzzes overnight. Only setback-caused heavy days push; cold days go to the feed. The threshold comes from the back-test (about 12 kWh of strips). During a Vacation trip the push is held and the strip kWh goes into the trip report.

What changes, in one list

  • History › Day and Week gain a What changed card. Each part has a badge, and the parts add up to the change.
  • Now: a morning line in the banner slot (lowest priority, 6–11 AM, dismissible).
  • Systems › Home: named loads in Where your energy goes, plus a Big loads disclosure and a naming sheet. The labels are Solstice's own and touch no device.
  • Systems › AC: a winter Strip heat card, advice only. One new alert switch, a push at most once a day at 10 AM, held during trips.
  • Server: four new nightly daily_metrics values (pool kWh, AC kWh, heating minutes, daily low), new load_bursts / load_labels tables (CREATE IF NOT EXISTS), and a one-time fetch of last winter's hourly temperatures.
  • Unchanged: no device writes, both Autopilots on Auto, no dollar figures, guests fail closed.
