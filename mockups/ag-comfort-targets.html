After warmer (or cooler) changes on 4 of 7 days, Solstice suggests moving that target 1°. It never changes it by itself.

Fix: changes group by day or night and by direction (warmer or cooler), not "within an hour and 1° of each other". Monday's 7:00 and 8:08 changes were left out today. A day counts once however many changes it has.

Changed: the suggestion moves the target 1° in that direction ("Make 79° the day target?") instead of rebuilding a band.

The rules

  • Your settings carry over: day = the middle of today's home band (78°), night = today's night setpoint (77°), pre-cool 2°, drift 1°. Nothing about the house changes on the day this ships.
  • Autopilot aims for the day target 7 AM – 10 PM and the night target overnight, pre-cools only on hot, sunny days while solar covers it, and never goes past target − pre-cool or target + drift.
  • Holds work as now: a change at the thermostat or in the app is kept for 2–8 hours.
  • Unchanged: Autopilot stays on Auto; control days, trims, the guards, presence and every other card.
