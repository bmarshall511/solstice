Mockup x · Freshness

Every live number says how old it is, and turns amber when it's stale.

Today the Now screen says "live" and "Powerwalls online" even when nothing new has arrived for an hour. The pool and AC cards say "Linked" whatever the age of their last read. These frames add an age to the places that already show status, and turn them amber past a set age. Every number is illustrative.

New: once the newest reading is 3 minutes old or more, three things change:

  • The Powerwalls chip turns amber and says how old the reading is.
  • The line under the greeting and the flow card read "as of 7:30 PM".
  • The battery orb and the flow scene dim, so a still screen doesn't look current.

The age comes from the reading's own timestamp, not from when the app last asked. The server's "Tesla not reporting" check moves to that timestamp too; today it looks at the fetch time (audit server #21).

The rules

  • Live reading (Now): amber at 3 minutes old. Grey "offline" when the app can't reach the server.
  • Pool: amber at 20 minutes while the pump is scheduled; age only otherwise.
  • Nest: amber at 20 minutes.
  • Ages count up on screen every second, from each reading's own timestamp, so they stay right even while a request is pending.
  • Unchanged: the layout of every screen, all numbers and charts, Settings › Connections (it already shows "read 8 min ago"), and guests' views (they get the same ages, no extra detail).
