The learning line opens the same "Your changes" sheet, for the pool.

The rules

  • Suggest only. Nothing is changed until you tap the yellow button; "Not now" hides that suggestion for 14 days.
  • 4 of the last 7 days, the same direction, within an hour of the same time (as the AC already does).
  • What counts for the pool: boosts and circuit runs from the app, and runs the hourly reads see outside the schedule. Speeds and the spa, lights, jets and blower never feed a suggestion.
  • Clear-up days and Autopilot's own speed-ups on spare solar are not your changes and never count.
  • Unchanged: holds, the hold banner, the planner, both Autopilots' modes, and every other card.
