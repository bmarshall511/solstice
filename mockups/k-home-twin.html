What's real vs mocked

From the app: the house, panels, wiring, sky and dash-flow shader (home scene), the pool outline (pool twin, rescaled to metres), the condenser and fan (thermal twin), and the label style.

Mocked: one synthetic day shaped like the home: about 71 kWh used, solar 37 kWh (about half), 2 × Powerwall 2 (27 kWh, 10 kW, reserve 20%), pump 153 W at 1,500 rpm 10a–7p, AC 2.0 kW pre-cooling 12–5 PM and coasting 5–9 PM. In the HUD, + means energy going into the Powerwalls or out to PEC. Where the pool and condenser sit is a placeholder (Q2). Frame 1 is seen live at 2:35 PM. Frames 2 and 3 are the same day seen at 10:40 PM, which is why they can replay 9 PM. Pool readings after 10 AM are missing on purpose, so replayed pool values fall back to the schedule.
