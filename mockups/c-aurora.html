Electric bills entered by you · stored locally

Tesla knows what flowed through your system. Your bill shows what the utility's meter recorded and what you actually paid. Putting the two side by side is how Solstice gets real dollar figures.

Drop your bill PDF here
It's read on your Mac and the fields below fill in for you to check. The PDF never leaves your computer.

Real savings, not estimates. Solstice compares your actual bill with what the same home usage (consumer_energy_imported_*) would have cost at your rates.
Meter reconciliation. The utility's kWh is checked against Tesla's grid import and export. Tesla is known to over-report import and export on some multi-Powerwall sites, and a big gap raises an alert.
Your rates, learned. Effective $/kWh, fixed charges and export credit come from your bills. This fills in the rate plan if Tesla's tariff data is missing.
Next-bill forecast. Midway through a billing period, Solstice projects the bill from live grid flows at your learned rates.
Payback tracker. Enter what the system cost once, and Solstice charts cumulative savings against it.
Year over year. Compare usage and cost by month, adjusted for weather using Open-Meteo temperatures (cooling degree-days).
