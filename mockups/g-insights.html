Mockup · Insights redesign

One page, four short panels, one 3D scene.

The long scroll becomes a sticky switcher: Today · Appliances · Planner · Home. Everything that's on Insights now is still here, just grouped and shorter.

The centrepiece is a three.js Day Ring: 24 hours around a circle, each bar stacked by what used the power (pool pump, AC, everything else) with your solar as a gold ribbon outside it. Drag to spin, tap an hour to read it. The modes morph the same ring live: as built, with the smarter pool schedule, with 8 more panels. The Planner sliders drive it too.

What's real

Pool hours come from ScreenLogic. AC is estimated from your heat model (kWh per degree above 80°F) until Nest is linked. "Everything else" is the remainder of Tesla's home load. Solar is real.

Bars here use sample numbers shaped like your September profile.
